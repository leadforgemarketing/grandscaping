Services

What we install

Six kinds of work, plus general landscaping when a garden needs levels, lawn and hard surfaces together.

Driveways

Block paving, resin and gravel, for homes and commercial premises. The base, edges and falls are set out so the finished drive is a clear place to park.

Patios and paving

Porcelain and sandstone patios, plus paths and paved links between the house, the drive and the garden.

Fencing

Decorative and boundary fencing, including Venetian fencing, for privacy and a tidy edge to the garden.

Aluminium pergolas

A slim aluminium pergola adds shade and structure over a patio, and gives the seating area a clear finish.

Decking

Decking creates a level terrace for sitting, including where the garden drops away from the house. It can stand alone or sit beside a patio.

Turf and artificial grass

Natural turf where you want a living lawn, or artificial grass where you want green underfoot with less mowing. The ground is prepared so the surface lies flat.
