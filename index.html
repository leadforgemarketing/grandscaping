Services

What we install

Driveways for domestic and commercial clients, plus patios, paving, fencing, pergolas, decking, turf and general landscaping.

Block paving driveways

A block paved drive gives a neat place to park and a clear entrance to the house. The pattern, edges and falls are set out as part of the job.

Resin driveways

Resin gives a smooth, continuous surface that suits both newer and older houses. It is a practical driveway finish once the base underneath is prepared.

Gravel driveways

Gravel is a natural-looking driveway and parking surface. A prepared base and firm edging keep the stone where it should be.

Porcelain patios

Porcelain is a consistent patio surface that is straightforward to keep clean. It suits a level seating area beside the house.

Sandstone patios

Sandstone brings natural colour and texture to a patio. It sits comfortably with lawns, borders and traditional houses.

Paving

Paths, steps and paved links between the house, the driveway and the garden, laid for comfortable walking and to move water away from the building.

Fencing

Decorative and boundary fencing, including Venetian fencing, for privacy and a tidy edge to the garden.

Aluminium pergolas

A slim aluminium pergola adds shade and structure over a patio, and gives the seating area a clear finish.

Decking

Decking creates a level terrace for sitting, including where the garden drops away from the house. It can stand alone or sit beside a patio.

Turf and artificial grass

Natural turf where you want a living lawn, or artificial grass where you want green underfoot with less mowing. The ground is prepared so the surface lies flat.

Landscaping

General landscaping when a garden needs several things at once: levels, lawn, borders and hard surfaces as one job.
